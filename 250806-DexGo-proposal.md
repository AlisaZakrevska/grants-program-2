### Project description

**DexGo** is a Move-to-Earn AR mobile game that motivates users to walk real-world routes, explore hidden urban locations, and earn rewards.  
This proposal focuses on integrating ICON smart contracts into DexGo to enable on-chain NFT sneakers and gameplay assets.

**Services and deliverables** include:
- Smart contract development and deployment
- NFT integration into gameplay
- Community onboarding via incentivized campaigns
- Marketing and growth to reach 30K+ monthly users

**Engagement**: DexGo will actively onboard ICON users and promote ICON-based NFTs in-game.  
**Total requested budget**: $30,000  
**ICON Ecosystem Benefit**: Integration of a real-world, mobile-first consumer product with gamified use of ICON blockchain. DexGo brings new users, transaction volume, and visibility to ICON through Web3 gaming and real-life activity.

### Maintenance

The DexGo team will maintain smart contracts, update gameplay logic, and monitor wallet/NFT functionality for at least 12 months after launch.  
Smart contract updates will follow ICON’s best practices for transparency and auditability.  
Regular in-game events, bug fixes, and community support via Discord and Telegram will ensure high engagement and performance.  

All core code will be open source and updated via GitHub with version control. Feedback and pull requests from the community will be encouraged.
